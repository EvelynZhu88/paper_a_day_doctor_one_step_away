Drop two square PNG icons in this folder so the PWA installs cleanly:

  icon-192.png  (192x192, used for the home-screen icon)
  icon-512.png  (512x512, used for splash screen and store listings)

Quick options:
  - Generate at https://realfavicongenerator.net (free, no signup)
  - Or any 512px PNG of your choice; resize a copy to 192px

Without these files, the PWA still works but the home-screen icon will be a
generic placeholder.
